# Smartphone & Tablet — Field Collection

**Use case:** collection | preservation | handoff  
**Environment:** ios | android | mobile  
**Risk:** caution

> **Field quick guide — authorized personnel only.** Device and operating-system behavior vary. When uncertain, **document the state and contact a qualified examiner before changing settings, power state, or connectivity.** Follow agency policy and legal authority.

## 60-second priority check

- [ ] Document where and how the device was found; photograph both sides and its **initial screen** if visible.
- [ ] Record **on/off**, **locked/unlocked**, charging status, visible time, visible notifications, damage, and connected accessories.
- [ ] Record make/model, case/identifiers if visible without manipulating or unlocking.
- [ ] If **off, leave it off**; do not plug it into power merely to see what is on it.
- [ ] If **on**, avoid rebooting or powering off without an approved plan; valuable access can be lost.
- [ ] If unlocked, promptly alert the examiner: the device might lock or restrict access.
- [ ] Follow *trained, agency-approved* network isolation and power-preservation procedures. Record any changes.
- [ ] Log custody and arrange prompt transfer.

## Why power and network state matter

- A powered-on device may have accessibility that is lost on reboot or power-down.
- Remote wipe or incoming network activity could change or destroy evidence.
- **Airplane mode is not a guarantee of isolation:** Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and other radios may remain active or reconnect.
- Faraday bags and RF enclosures can fail to fully shield a device and can drain its battery faster. Use only tested equipment and an approved plan for power; do not run a cable through an RF enclosure if it defeats shielding.

## Do not

- Guess or repeatedly try a passcode; device security protections may be triggered.
- Open conversations, browse apps, respond to notifications, or explore files out of curiosity.
- Remove or switch SIM/eSIM components without examiner direction.
- Connect a device to an unknown computer, charger, or data accessory.
- Turn on an off device, especially one that is wet, overheated, swollen, or damaged.
- Change network settings unless authorized and trained for that device and situation.

## Escalate immediately if

- The screen is unlocked and a qualified examiner is not yet on scene.
- The device is damaged, wet, overheating, or otherwise unsafe.
- There is a credible remote-wipe concern.
- A Faraday enclosure cannot maintain its intended isolation or power needs.
- Multiple linked devices, watches, computers, or security keys may be relevant.

## Handoff details

Tell the receiving examiner **exactly** what the device was doing when found, whether it was unlocked, what you changed, whether it was isolated, how power was maintained, and every custody transfer.
