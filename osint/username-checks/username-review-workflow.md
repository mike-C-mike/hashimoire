# Username Review — Public Presence and Impersonation

**Use case:** authorized account-inventory review | brand impersonation | exposure reduction  
**Risk:** caution

## Purpose

Determine whether **an account or handle you control or are authorized to review** appears publicly elsewhere, and whether any candidate pages may misrepresent that identity. A repeated username is a **candidate match**, never automatic identity attribution.

## 1. Record authority and search terms

```text
Review objective:
Authorized subject / organization:
Known verified accounts:
Handle variations:
Allowed platforms and public sources:
Start time (UTC):
Out of scope: private accounts, private messages, authentication probes,
             contacts/friends, location tracking, direct engagement
```

For personal exposure work, obtain the individual's agreement. Do not extend the exercise into investigation of their friends, relatives, or minors.

## 2. Review ordinary public search results

Use a public search engine with a **small, justified** set of exact queries, for example:

```text
"examplehandle"
"@examplehandle"
"examplehandle" site:github.com
"examplehandle" site:reddit.com
"Example Brand" "official"
"Example Brand" "support"
```

Site operators are search-engine-dependent and results may be stale. Do not assume absence from results means no account exists.

## 3. Validate candidate accounts

For each candidate, note **publicly visible** information necessary to answer the question:

| Observation | What it may support | What it cannot prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Same handle | Candidate for review | Same owner across platforms |
| Direct link from a verified official site | Stronger ownership link | Historical ownership or ongoing control |
| Same photo or biography | Possible account association | Definitive identification |
| Different dates / content | Alternative explanation | That account is fraudulent |
| Claim of official support | Possible impersonation concern | Malicious behavior without corroboration |

Classify conclusions as **Confirmed (supported by verified crosslink/owner), Likely, Possible, Unrelated, or Impersonation suspected**. If you lack reliable corroboration, use *Possible*.

## 4. Example (entirely fictional)

```text
Known official account: @examplebrand
Candidate: @examplebrand_support
Public evidence: profile claims "official customer help"
Verified official website lists only @examplebrand
Assessment: possible impersonation; ownership unconfirmed
Recommended action: ask authorized brand owner to review / use platform reporting
Do not do: direct message, bait, account-access testing
```

A lookalike name is not itself proof of fraud. Preserve only the minimum screenshot/URL and observation time needed.

## 5. Record and minimize

```text
Finding ID:
Platform / public URL:
Observed UTC time:
Precisely observed facts:
Verification source:
Confidence and alternatives:
Risk to owner:
Suggested cleanup or platform report:
Stored evidence reference / retention:
```

Avoid collecting friend lists, phone numbers, family relationships, or exact addresses when they are not needed. Never paste personal case data into Hashimoire.

## 6. Actions that may provide value

- Remove unnecessary public cross-links for accounts the owner controls.
- Correct public brand/organization contact pages.
- Enable appropriate account security controls.
- Report a suspected impersonating account through platform processes.
- Record unresolved candidates as **unverified**, rather than building a speculative identity map.

## Limitations

Search indexes are selective, public profiles change, handles can be reassigned, and screenshots can be manipulated. Attribution requires multiple independent, reliable sources and a legitimate investigative purpose.
